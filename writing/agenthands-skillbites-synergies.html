AgentHands x Skillbites — Partnership Synergies

March 2026 · 8 min read

One company teaches AI. The other builds it. Same founder, same tech stack, different markets. That's the opportunity.

What Each Brings

AgentHands is an AI agency — consulting, automation, and custom platforms for B2B operators and founders. Revenue comes from custom projects ($5k–$500k+). Pre-launch with 40 qualified leads.

Skillbites is a micro-learning platform with 256+ practical life skills courses aimed at busy adults 25–40. $19.99 one-time lifetime access. Active, with ~80 courses live across 14 series. Built on the same stack: Next.js, Drizzle, Supabase, Stripe.

1. Skillbites Becomes AgentHands' Education Arm

AgentHands lists education and training services — executive briefings, AI readiness workshops, corporate programs — but has no course content built yet.

Skillbites already has 45+ AI courses across five series: AI Demystified, AI App Builder, LLM Engineering, Building AI Products, and Advanced AI Engineering.

The play: License or co-brand Skillbites AI content as curriculum for AgentHands' corporate education offerings. AgentHands sells the workshop ($5k–$50k). Skillbites provides the material. AgentHands gets a productized training offering without building from scratch. Skillbites gets B2B revenue without building a sales team.

2. Corporate/B2B Tier for Skillbites

AgentHands has the sales pipeline and B2B relationships that Skillbites lacks. New pricing tiers sold through AgentHands:

  • Individual: $19.99 one-time — full course library, lifetime access
  • Team (5–25 seats): $149/team — all courses + progress tracking per member
  • Enterprise (25+ seats): $499–$2,499 — custom bundles, admin dashboard, completion reports
  • Workshop Add-On: $5k–$50k — live facilitation by AgentHands + Skillbites curriculum

AgentHands currently has no productized recurring revenue. This could be it.

3. AI Chat Technology Transfer

AgentHands built a sophisticated AI chat intake system — slot-based conversation state machine, lead qualification, streaming responses. That same tech could power a Skillbites onboarding chatbot, learning path builder, or in-course AI tutor.

AgentHands gets a showcase. Skillbites gets a feature that would take weeks to build solo.

4. Cross-Marketing Loop

AgentHands → Skillbites: Point prospects to Skillbites AI courses as proof of education expertise. "We don't just build AI — we teach it." Client onboarding includes Skillbites access as a value-add.

Skillbites → AgentHands: AI course completers see "Want AI built for your business? Talk to AgentHands." Podcast episodes on AI topics include AgentHands CTA. Email sequences segment AI-interested users for warm intros.

Same founder writing about AI from both angles builds authority fast. Skillbites content says "here's how it works." AgentHands content says "here's how we build it."

5. Shared Production Infrastructure

Nearly identical stacks. Concrete reuse:

  • Skillbites' Remotion video pipeline → AgentHands marketing videos
  • AgentHands' chat intake system → Skillbites onboarding and recommendations
  • Shared component library across both sites
  • Skillbites' ElevenLabs audio pipeline → AgentHands demo content

6. Joint Podcast

Skillbites is building "Skillbites Daily" — a 5-minute daily micro-podcast. AgentHands needs thought leadership content.

The simplest version: Skillbites Daily covers AI topics, each episode ends with "Need this built? agenthands.io." Low effort, immediate cross-pollination.

The ambitious version: a companion show — "Built With AI" — where each episode covers a real problem, how Skillbites teaches it, and how AgentHands would build it. Education-to-implementation pipeline in audio form.

7. Case Study Flywheel

AgentHands builds something for a client. Skillbites writes a "How X Works" course about the pattern. AgentHands uses that course for the next client's training. That client sees the course, refers others to Skillbites. Skillbites users discover AgentHands. Each engagement creates content. Each piece of content creates leads.

Quick Wins

  • Cross-link the sites. AgentHands education section links to Skillbites AI courses. Skillbites AI courses link to AgentHands.
  • Shared email segments. Skillbites captures emails at the free-course limit. AI-interested leads get warm intro to AgentHands.
  • "Powered by" badge. AgentHands education page says "Curriculum by Skillbites." Instant credibility for both.
  • Bundle pitch. Next AgentHands prospect gets Skillbites team access included in the proposal.

Risks to Watch

Brand tone gap. AgentHands is quiet, calm, institutional. Skillbites is punchy, curious, opinionated. Joint content needs a clear voice decision.

Audience gap. B2B executives vs. B2C 25-year-olds. The AI content overlaps but the framing differs. May need separate packaging of the same core material.

Price perception. $19.99 consumer product alongside $50k consulting engagements. Frame it as "we democratize AI education" not "our courses are cheap." The $19.99 is the gateway; the consulting is the destination.

The Big Bet

Package Skillbites AI courses into a corporate training product that AgentHands sells to its clients. AgentHands gets a productized education offering instead of custom-building every time. Skillbites gets B2B revenue without building a sales team. And it creates a natural pipeline: learn on Skillbites, implement with AgentHands.

One company teaches. The other builds. Same mission. Different entry points.
